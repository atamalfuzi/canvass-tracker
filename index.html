Canvass Tracker

Door-knocking tools for a 2026 municipal campaign in Coquitlam, BC

Volunteers record each door on their phone. The campaign team sees the results on a map next to the polling stations, plans walking routes, and follows up with undecided voters and lawn-sign requests. The real version stores everything in a Google Sheet through a small Apps Script backend.

This copy runs on made-up data in your browser. Nothing you enter leaves it.
